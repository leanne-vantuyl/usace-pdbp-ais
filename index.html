Initiation Phase: Work Acceptance (PROC1000)

Receive work request from Primary Stakeholder or HQUSACE directive
Initial request should include preliminary scope information. Document the requesting party, nature of work, and date received.
Forward work request to DPM or Work Acceptance Official
Notify the DPM promptly. Do not begin any work activities prior to DPM acknowledgment.
Assign work request to appropriate PgM or Senior PM
Per local SOPs, PgM role may be delegated to a Senior PM. Document the assignment.
[DECISION] Is work within the USACE Activity's AOR or Mission Assignment?
Reference ER 5-1-10 and ER 1140-1-211. Identify statutory authority. If YES → Step 7. If NO → Step 5.

Planning Phase: PMP Development (PROC2000)

Initiate PMP development concurrent with project authorization
Begin immediately upon work acceptance — do not wait for final funding confirmation. Reference REF8005G for all required sections.
Define PMP structure and all required subsidiary plans
Required plans: Risk Management, Quality Management, Communications, Safety, Acquisition, Schedule, Resource Estimate.
Draft project description, purpose, and authorization basis
State what is authorized, by whom, and under what authority. Include statutory basis, appropriation source, and authorized scope.

Execution Phase: Project Delivery (PROC3000)

Confirm execution authorization: approved PMP, confirmed funds, full PDT staffing
Verify: approved PMP, funds confirmed, PDT fully staffed with named individuals. Issue formal notice to proceed.
Execute the project per the approved PMP baseline
Each member executes their scope per the approved plan. Deviations must be identified and reported immediately.
Conduct schedule updates and EVM performance measurement at minimum monthly
Update the schedule monthly minimum. Measure EVM metrics where required. Document trends.

Closeout Phase: Project Closeout (PROC4000)

Confirm all deliverables complete and formally accepted
Verify every deliverable has been completed, inspected, and accepted. Document the non-federal sponsor's formal written acceptance of all project outputs.
Confirm all contracts closed or in formal closeout status
Verify: final invoices paid, performance evaluations submitted, all modifications complete, and contractor disputes resolved or in formal dispute resolution.
Reconcile all project accounts and unliquidated obligations (ULOs)
Confirm all expenditures captured, all ULOs justified or deobligated, and the project financial record is complete and accurate.
